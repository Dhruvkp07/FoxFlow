🦊 About FoxFlow

FoxFlow v1.0 — Productivity Intelligence Platform

Track your focus, apps, websites, and input activity. Build streaks, achieve goals, and unlock AI-powered insights about your work patterns.

Features
  • 👁️ Eye tracking with focus detection
  • 💻 App usage monitoring
  • 🌐 Website visit tracking
  • ⌨️ Keyboard & mouse activity
  • 🍅 Pomodoro timer with auto-blocking
  • 🎯 Custom goals & streak system
  • 🚫 Website blocker
  • 📈 Daily, weekly, monthly reports
  • 🤖 AI-powered behavior analysis
🚀 Quick Start Guide
  1. Click "Start Tracking" on the Dashboard to begin
  2. Set up Goals to build productivity streaks
  3. Use the Pomodoro Timer for focused work sessions
  4. Add distracting sites to the Blocker
  5. Check Reports to see your daily progress
  6. Ask the AI for personalized productivity tips
